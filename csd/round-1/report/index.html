Round 1 results: what Altadena neighbors said about the rules for single-family homes

What Round 1 was for. LA County Planning is reviewing Altadena’s Community Standards District (CSD), the part of the County’s zoning code with building standards specific to Altadena, and is considering possible changes. Nothing has been decided. The County formed a focus group to discuss them, and Altagether holds a seat on it. Altagether is gathering neighbors’ views in three rounds, one for each set of rules the group discusses, so the County hears from neighbors block by block.

Round 1 covered single-family homes: four changes the County is considering to yards, height, front fences and parking. 616 neighbors answered between October 3 and 12, 2026.

The report shows the results zone by zone and by census tract, the main themes from neighbors’ comments in our words, and the questions neighbors raised that only the County can answer.

Who this report is shared with

  • LA County Planning and its Altadena focus group, on October 14, 2026, as Altagether’s input for the review of Altadena’s CSD. We have asked the County to answer neighbors’ questions in writing.
  • Everyone who answered and left an email address, and Altagether’s block captains, who share it with their zones.
  • Community organizations that helped neighbors take part, and anyone who visits this page.

Every answer, for and against, is counted. No one’s individual answer or comment appears in the report, and zones with fewer than 5 answers are combined for privacy.
