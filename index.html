Neighborhood input

Altagether brings what Altadena neighbors think to the people making decisions, block by block. Surveys go out through Altagether’s block captains and local community organizations, and the results come back to you.

Altadena’s building rules

LA County Planning is reviewing Altadena’s Community Standards District (CSD), the part of the County’s zoning code with building standards specific to Altadena, and is considering possible changes. Nothing has been decided. The County formed a focus group to discuss the rules, and Altagether holds a seat on it. Altagether is gathering neighbors’ views in three rounds, one for each set of rules the group discusses.

Round 1 · Yards, height, fences, parkingClosed

Single-family homes. Open October 3 to 12, 2026. 616 neighbors answered.

The County’s disaster recovery ordinances, including the one for the Eaton Fire. The focus group is scheduled to take these up in December. Your block captain will send the link when it opens.
